## 1. The challenge

> **If your team were a fintech startup, what new payment service would you launch to create the greatest positive impact for people, businesses, and society?**

The solution must solve a **meaningful, payment-related problem** for a **clearly identified group of customers**.

Suggested areas (examples only; any idea about the future of payments is accepted):

- Financial inclusion and access to financial services
- Cybersecurity and secure digital payments
- Digital identity
- Merchant services
- Artificial intelligence in payments
- Cross-border payments
- Digital currencies and blockchain-based services
- Sustainable commerce

## 2. Team roles

Split the work by each member's skills. These are areas of responsibility, not fixed job titles.

| Role | Responsible for |

| **Product & Customer Experience Manager** | User needs, customer journey, value proposition, UX/UI |

| **Risk, Security & Compliance Officer** | Security, fraud prevention, privacy, compliance, impact assessment |

## 3. What the team must develop

The solution has to cover all eight areas. They are also the most likely judging criteria, so treat this table as the master checklist.

| # | Area | What's expected | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Meaningful problem** | The payment-related problem and why it matters | Product & CX + Analyst |
| 2 | **Target market** | Intended customers and their needs | Product & CX |
| 3 | **Market & competitor analysis** | Existing services and gaps to improve on | Financial & Market Analyst |
| 4 | **Differentiated payment service** | How it works and what makes it distinctive | Product & CX + Tech Lead |
| 5 | **Prototype** | Show how the service works in practice | Technology & Innovation Lead |

## 5. Analysis

### The prototype is needed now
The application video has to introduce **the prototype**, so a demoable MVP is needed by **9 Nov 2026**, not just an idea. From today (6 Nov) that leaves about three days. A clickable mockup or a narrow, working core flow is the realistic target. Keep the scope to one user journey shown end to end.

### Pitch to the organiser
HPS is a Moroccan payments-technology company. Its PowerCARD platform covers card issuing, merchant acquiring, switching and payment processing for banks and processors, and it is used across Africa and beyond. Ideas that plug into that kind of infrastructure (cards, wallets, merchant acceptance, switching, fraud detection) and serve African markets will probably resonate with the jury. They also make the internship angle more credible.

### Themes that suit a fast prototype
| Theme | Why it works for a 3-day MVP |
|---|---|
| Merchant services | Simple merchant or customer flows (QR pay, payment links, micro-merchant dashboards) are quick to mock up and easy to demo |
| AI in payments | A fraud or credit-scoring demo on synthetic data shows "innovation" visibly |
| Financial inclusion | Strong impact story (area 8): unbanked users, USSD and offline payments, informal merchants |
| Cross-border payments | Clear pain point (fees, delays) for remittances and intra-African trade, though regulation (area 7) is heavier |
| Digital currencies / blockchain | Eye-catching, but harder to make credible on regulation and sustainability in 3 days |

### What a strong submission looks like
- One sharp problem with a real, named customer segment (for example, informal merchants in Moroccan medinas), not a generic "the unbanked"
- At least one number showing the size of the problem, and one KPI for measuring impact
- An honest competitor scan (M-Pesa, Wave, CashPlus, CIH Pay, Chaabi Pay, etc. as relevant) plus a clear differentiator
- A revenue model (fees, SaaS, interchange share) that matches the target customer's ability to pay
- Security and compliance designed in from the start: KYC/AML, data privacy (Morocco's Law 09-08), Bank Al-Maghrib payment-institution rules, PCI DSS if cards are involved

### What the guidelines leave out (ask the organisers)
- Team size limits (minimum and maximum) and whether members must come from different schools or majors
- Required video length, format and upload size
- Whether a mockup counts as a prototype or working code is expected
- Event dates and format after the application (pitch day, mentoring, finals)
- Judging criteria and weights, and how the Audience Choice vote works
