# User interviews

Target 5–10 people from the chosen segment. 10 minutes each, in person or by phone.

## Questions
1. How do you pay or get paid today?
2. What is the most annoying part? When did it last cause a problem?
3. What have you tried to fix it? Why didn't it work?
4. If [our solution] existed, would you use it? What would stop you?
5. Would you pay for it? How much?
